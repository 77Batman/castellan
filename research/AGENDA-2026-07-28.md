## What the firm will NOT do, and why

Named now so no one spends compute discovering it later.

| Not doing | Reason |
|---|---|
| Cross-sectional fundamental equity factors | **Foreclosed, not deprioritized.** No PIT fundamentals; ~78% of firms restate audited annual revenue within 400 days [cited — S&P Global MI]. Inadmissible at Gate 0. Any backtest of this is uninterpretable. |
| Anything intraday, market-making, or latency-sensitive | No tick or order-book history for equities. Minimum equity holding period one trading day (§3.3). |
| Single-name short-driven equity strategies | No borrow data. Permitted only with ≥4%/yr specials assumed, hard-to-borrow excluded, and a constraint sensitivity — which has been documented to halve live performance [cited — DB *Seven Sins*]. Most such edges will not survive it. |
| Kitchen-sink ML feature mining | Mechanically incompatible with DSR ≥ 0.95. The hurdle rises roughly as √(2·ln N); a method whose whole approach is to make N enormous cannot clear it. Not an aesthetic objection. |
| Macro narrative forecasting | §8's explicit warning to Pod C. "We predict macro" is a red-flag phrase, not a thesis. |
| Any real capital | Gate 2. Principal only, in writing. |

## Throughput commitment

**Target ≥ 2 hypotheses reaching a terminal verdict (Gate 1 PASS / KILL /
PARK-WITH-TRIGGER) per calendar month**, reported in every Monthly Letter whether
or not it is met. A pipeline that grows without resolving is reported as a failure.

Sprint 1 will produce **zero** terminal verdicts. It produces pre-registrations and
a data layer. Stated plainly so the first Monthly Letter is not a surprise.
